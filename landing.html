US COMPLIANCE

Questions about U.S. verification compliance

Does US-EVS prepare or submit Form I-9 records?

No. US-EVS provides a secure workplace workflow for employer-side verification records, but the employer must still complete the official USCIS Form I-9 and related onboarding paperwork as required by the applicable federal and state rules.

Can US-EVS automate E-Verify or right-to-work checks?

US-EVS can support employer-side verification workflows, audit logging, and document retention for U.S. hiring checks, but it does not replace the employer's legal responsibility to follow current E-Verify, I-9, and local compliance requirements.

Does a US-EVS reference confirm FCRA compliance or background check rules?

No. A US-EVS reference confirms that a verification record exists in the platform. It does not certify compliance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act, state consumer-protection laws, or any employer's independent screening program.
